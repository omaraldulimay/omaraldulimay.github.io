The problem

With protein powder and bars, the mistakes often sit in the data and in the advertising copy.

  1. Nutrition values are missing or do not add up
  2. An allergen is in the ingredients but not in the declaration
  3. Advertising claims the EU health claims regulation does not allow

The solution

  1. Rules in Python check gaps, contradictions, allergens and nutrition claims, free and always the same
  2. The AI sees only suspicious sentences and suggests an allowed wording
  3. One click applies the fix and the traffic light switches

Decisions

  • 0001

    Rules first, AI only for language

    Python calculates and compares exactly and reproducibly. In return, every new rule needs code.

  • 0002

    Prefilter for allowed claims

    Python checks authorised EU claims and nutrition claims such as High Protein itself and never sends them to the models. That saves errors. In return, the list has to be maintained.

  • 0003

    The model only picks a rule ID

    Legal basis and text come from the code, not from the model. In return, the texts sound standardised.

  • 0004

    Answers in a cache

    Every AI answer is stored, no sentence is checked twice. In return, a changed text is checked again.

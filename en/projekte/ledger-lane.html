The problem

A marketplace collects money for many brands. Money must never be booked twice, halfway or lost.

  1. Customer clicks Pay twice
  2. Server crashes in the middle of a booking
  3. Bank and accounting show different numbers

    How every cent stays clean

    1. Double entry bookkeeping: every booking sums to exactly 0
    2. Idempotency: same request twice, still only one booking
    3. Immutable: errors are reversed, never deleted
    4. Outbox: every booking is guaranteed to land in Kafka
    5. Key based access: shop books, finance pays out, audit reads
    6. Euro and dollar: each currency balances to 0 on its own

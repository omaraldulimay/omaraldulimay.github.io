The problem

A helpdesk a company sells as a subscription to many customers. Every customer gets exactly the speed they pay for, whatever the others do.

The result

  1. New customers ready with one call, large customers with their own database
  2. Every plan gets its speed: 20, 100 or 500 requests per second
  3. A rush at one customer: the others keep answering in 6.3 ms

    Decisions

    • 0001

      Shared database for small customers, own one for enterprise

      Small plans run cheaply together, large customers get performance of their own. Both with the same code.

    • 0002

      The rules live in the database

      Every table knows its customer. New features are multi tenant from day one, with no extra work in the code.

    • 0003

      Customer and plan come from the login

      A signed token tells every request at once who is asking and which plan applies.

    • 0004

      Speed per customer in memory

      One counter per customer and plan catches 95.6 % of a flood before it reaches the database.
